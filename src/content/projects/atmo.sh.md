atmo.sh shows the SSH public keys any account publishes as `sh.tangled.publicKey` records, and lets you manage your own after signing in with OAuth from any PDS.

Look anyone up at `atmo.sh/<handle>` or `atmo.sh/<did>`. Profile pages carry [at-tags](https://tangled.org/chrisshank.com/at-tags) pointing back to the records, plus a share card rendered for each profile.
