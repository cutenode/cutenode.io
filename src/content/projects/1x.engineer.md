A website about what a 1x engineer _really_ is. It started as a reply to the 10x engineer discourse on Twitter, and it's styled with NES.css because it's pretty.

Know a 1x trait that's missing? Open a PR, keep it free of blame, and don't leave any 1x engineers out. There's also a badge if you want to show off your 1x status in your own README.
