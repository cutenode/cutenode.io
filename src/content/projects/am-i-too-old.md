A tiny CLI with one job: telling you whether you're too old to learn to code. All you need is Node.js.

[Code Like a Girl](https://code.likeagirl.io/) shared [an article by Chuck Phipps](https://medium.com/startup-grind/i-am-enter-your-age-here-is-that-too-late-to-become-a-developer-58e926799af4) about learning to code later in life, and this repeats its message from your terminal.
