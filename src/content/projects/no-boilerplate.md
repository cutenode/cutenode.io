Sometimes a website only needs to say one word: No. This is a tiny HTML starter for shipping exactly that kind of site, and [noboilerplate.com](https://noboilerplate.com) is what it looks like out of the box.

Clone it, point the remote at your own repo, and publish. Everything you'd want to change lives in `index.html` and `index.css`.
