Sometimes a website only needs to say one word: No. This is a tiny HTML starter for shipping exactly that kind of site on GitHub Pages or any static host.

Clone it, point the remote at your own repo, and publish. Change "No" to "Yes" (or anything else) in `index.html`, and there's a hidden message further down the page you can edit or delete.
