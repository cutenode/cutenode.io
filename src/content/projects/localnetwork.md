Local Network is tools for nightlife, for DJs and the people who book them. Each tool works on its own, and they share an Atmosphere account, a lexicon tree (`computer.localnetwork.*`) and file formats.

The website at [localnetwork.computer](https://localnetwork.computer) is SvelteKit, locallibrary is Rust and the extension is Svelte on Manifest V3. They all live in one repo.
